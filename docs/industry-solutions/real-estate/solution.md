# iSift for Real Estate

Public page: `#/industry/real-estate` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Sales agencies, property management teams, buyer's agents, developers' sales offices.

## 2. Pain points
- Vendors, buyers and tenants will not install apps or create accounts.
- Agency paperwork needs signatures, copies and records kept for years.
- Meetings, mail, documents and signing are split across four tools.
- Appraisals, inspections and viewings are booked by phone and text.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Guests join free from a link, in the browser.
- Built-in PDF editing and signing for agreements and applications.
- AI minutes drafted, sent only after a person approves, for vendor and landlord meetings.
- Booking pages for appraisals and viewings; team booking and audit log on Business.
- Invitations from the agent's own email address.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Written agency agreement signed before commission can be claimed; copy to vendor (state rules differ).
- Cooling-off periods and state-specific forms and disclosures.
- Record retention varies by state (e.g. Vic 7 years; NSW trust records 3 years minimum).
- Trust account rules (accounting software stays separate).
- AML/CTF obligations for real estate agents (check AUSTRAC for current start dates).
- Consent for recording calls (state surveillance-device laws).

## 5. Gaps to build
- Signing audit trail, signing order, reusable templates, state forms.
- Retention settings, legal hold, full client-file export.
- Recording consent prompt and consent log.
- Property-linked records (address + contact).
- CRM connectors (VaultRE, Rex, Agentbox, Console) and listing portals.
- Open-home / inspection booking flow.

## 6. Starter templates
Appraisal meeting agenda and minutes; vendor update; tenant inspection booking; agency agreement signing pack; rental application.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
VaultRE, Rex, Agentbox, Console, REA / Domain lead feeds, Zapier.
