# iSift for Healthcare

Public page: `#/industry/healthcare` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
GP and allied health clinics, psychologists, physios, dental, aged care coordinators, NDIS providers.

## 2. Pain points
- Patients struggle with apps and logins for telehealth.
- Clinical notes and admin are written twice.
- Consent forms and referrals need signatures.
- Reminders and no-shows cost revenue.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Patients join free from a link in the browser.
- AI minutes drafted for clinician review; nothing is sent until approved.
- PDF signing for consent and intake forms.
- Booking pages and calendar sync; mail from the clinic's address.
- Business plan: SSO and audit log.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles; health information is sensitive information.
- My Health Records Act obligations where connected.
- State health records laws; retention commonly long (adults often 7 years; minors longer).
- Telehealth professional guidance (identity, consent, emergency plan).
- Data location and breach notification (Notifiable Data Breaches scheme).
- Recording needs explicit consent.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Waiting room and clinician-controlled admit.
- Australian data hosting statement and security documentation.
- Consent capture and log; retention settings.
- Practice management connectors (Best Practice, Medical Director, Cliniko, Halaxy).
- Medicare billing is out of scope; link to existing systems.

## 6. Starter templates
Telehealth consent; new patient intake; referral summary; care-team case conference minutes; NDIS service agreement.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Cliniko, Halaxy, Best Practice, Medical Director, HealthEngine/HotDoc booking, Zapier.

## 8. Landing page copy
**Headline:** Telehealth without the app.
**Subhead:** Patients click a link and join. Notes are drafted, you approve.
- No app or account for patients
- Consent forms signed before the call
- AI notes never leave without your approval
