# iSift for Government

Public page: `#/industry/government` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Local councils, small agencies, statutory bodies, public libraries and community services.

## 2. Pain points
- Public consultation needs accessible, no-account participation.
- Meetings are subject to records and transparency rules.
- Procurement demands security evidence and local data hosting.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Citizens join free from a link.
- Webinars for consultation and information sessions.
- Captions on the free plan; approved AI minutes.
- Business plan: SSO and audit log.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- State/Commonwealth records and archives acts; open-meeting rules for councils.
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.x; DDA expectations).
- Security frameworks: ISM / IRAP assessment for Commonwealth use; state equivalents.
- Data sovereignty and hosting in Australia.
- Freedom of information and privacy law.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Australian hosting commitment and IRAP/SOC 2 roadmap.
- Accessibility conformance report.
- Public-meeting recording, transcript publishing and retention controls.
- Live translation / Auslan interpreter support.
- Procurement pack (security questionnaire, data processing terms).

## 6. Starter templates
Council meeting agenda and minutes; community consultation webinar; submissions booking; FOI request log.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Microsoft 365, Objective / TRIM records systems, Zapier.

## 8. Landing page copy
**Headline:** Open meetings, open to everyone.

- Webinars for consultation
