# iSift for Professional Services

Public page: `#/industry/professional-services` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Law firms, consultancies, recruiters, architects and designers, marketing agencies, IT service providers.

## 2. Pain points
- Client confidentiality; meetings, documents and signing in separate tools.
- Billable meetings need accurate notes and action items.
- Engagement letters and NDAs need signatures.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Clients join free from a link.
- AI minutes and action items drafted for approval.
- PDF editing and signing for engagement letters and NDAs.
- Co-edit documents and slides in the meeting; booking pages for consultations.
- Business plan: SSO, audit log, priority support.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Legal professional privilege and confidentiality; trust accounting for law firms.
- Records retention (typically 7 years for legal and tax).
- Privacy Act; client data location.
- Recruiters: candidate privacy and record retention.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Matter / client-linked files; time-recording export.
- Signing audit trail and templates.
- Connectors: LEAP, Actionstep, Xero, HubSpot, Bullhorn.
- Recording and transcript retention controls.

## 6. Starter templates
Client intake; engagement letter; NDA; project kickoff; candidate interview.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Xero, HubSpot, LEAP, Actionstep, Bullhorn, Zapier.
