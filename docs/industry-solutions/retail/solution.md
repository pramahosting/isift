# iSift for Retail and E-commerce

Public page: `#/industry/retail` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Independent retailers, e-commerce brands, wholesalers, franchise groups.

## 2. Pain points
- Supplier negotiations and buying meetings across time zones.
- Customer consultations (fit, design, custom orders) need booking and follow-up.
- Staff and franchisee meetings across stores.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Booking pages for personal shopping and consultations; guests join free.
- Webinars for product launches and live demos.
- Share screens and co-edit order sheets during supplier calls.
- Mail from the store's own address; PDF signing for wholesale terms.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Australian Consumer Law (claims, refunds, pricing in advertising).
- Privacy Act for customer data; spam and marketing-consent rules.
- Franchising Code of Conduct for franchise groups.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Shopify / WooCommerce / Square connectors.
- Live shopping / product showcase mode.
- Payment on booking (deposits).
- Reminder SMS.

## 6. Starter templates
Supplier negotiation; wholesale terms; custom order consultation; product launch webinar; franchisee briefing.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Shopify, WooCommerce, Square, Xero, Mailchimp, Zapier.

## 8. Landing page copy
**Headline:** Turn browsers into booked consultations.
**Subhead:** Customers book and join from a link; no app, no account.
- Booking page with reminders
