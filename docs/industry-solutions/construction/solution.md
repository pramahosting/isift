# iSift for Construction and Trades

Public page: `#/industry/construction` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Builders, renovators, architects' site teams, electricians, plumbers and other trades, project managers.

## 2. Pain points
- Site meetings with owners, subcontractors and certifiers on mobile.
- Variations and quotes must be signed before work proceeds.
- Photos, plans and decisions scattered across texts and email.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Clients and subcontractors join free from a link on any phone.
- PDF signing for quotes, variations and contracts.
- Booking pages for quotes and site visits.
- Co-edit schedules and punch lists in the meeting; AI action items you approve.
- Mail from the business's own address.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Home building contracts and variation rules (state-based); written, signed variations.
- Licensing and insurance records; WHS site records.
- Progress payment / security of payment laws.
- Records retention for warranty and defect periods.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Mobile-first join and low-bandwidth mode.
- Photo / plan annotation during calls.
- Variation and quote templates; audit trail.
- Connectors: Buildxact, Procore, ServiceM8, Xero.

## 6. Starter templates
Site meeting minutes; variation approval; quote acceptance; subcontractor onboarding; handover checklist.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Xero, ServiceM8, Buildxact, Procore, Zapier.
