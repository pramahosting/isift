# iSift for Nonprofit

Public page: `#/industry/nonprofit` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Charities, community groups, clubs, volunteer-run organisations, associations.

## 2. Pain points
- Tight budgets; per-user pricing for volunteers is costly.
- Volunteers and members use many different email and calendar systems.
- Board meetings need minutes and resolutions on record.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Free plan: 25 people, 90 minutes. Guests join free.
- Price follows room size, from A$2 per host, not per volunteer.
- Connect Google, Microsoft, Yahoo and iCloud calendars.
- Webinars for fundraising and info nights; approved AI minutes for board meetings.
- PDF signing for volunteer agreements.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- ACNC governance standards; AGM and board minute records.
- Fundraising and privacy rules; donor data protection.
- Working With Children checks documentation where relevant.
- Retention of governing documents and financial records.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Nonprofit discount or verified pricing.
- Board pack and resolution templates, voting record.
- Connectors: Donorbox, Xero, Mailchimp, Salesforce Nonprofit.
- Member / volunteer roster and sign-up pages.

## 6. Starter templates
Board meeting agenda and minutes; AGM notice and proxy; volunteer agreement; fundraising event webinar.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Xero, Mailchimp, Donorbox, Google Workspace, Zapier.
