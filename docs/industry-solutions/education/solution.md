# iSift for Education

Public page: `#/industry/education` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Independent schools, RTOs and colleges, universities' small teams, tutors, music and language schools.

## 2. Pain points
- 40-minute free limits break lessons.
- Parents and students will not create accounts.
- Slides, worksheets and meetings live in separate tools.
- Permission forms and enrolments need signatures.

## 3. How iSift fits
- 90-minute free meetings; Standard has no time limit.
- Guests join free from a link.
- Co-edit notes, sheets, slides or whiteboard during class.
- Several people can share screens at once.
- Webinars for open days and info nights; PDF signing for permission forms.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Child safety and privacy of minors; state child-safe standards.
- Parental consent for recording students.
- Records for accredited training (RTO/ASQA rules) including assessment evidence.
- Data location and student data privacy.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Waiting room, mute-all, breakout rooms, attendance report.
- Parent/guardian consent flow and recording controls.
- LMS connectors (Canvas, Moodle, Google Classroom) and SIS.
- Assessment evidence export.

## 6. Starter templates
Parent-teacher interview booking; class session with whiteboard; excursion permission form; enrolment pack; open-day webinar.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Google Classroom, Canvas, Moodle, Compass, Zapier.

## 8. Landing page copy
**Headline:** Lessons that don't stop at 40 minutes.
**Subhead:** 90 free minutes, students join from a link, work together on the same page.
- No accounts for students or parents
- Whiteboard and docs inside the class
- Permission forms signed online
**CTA:** Start free at isift.au
