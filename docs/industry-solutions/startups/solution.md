# iSift for Startups

Public page: `#/industry/startups` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Founders and small teams, accelerators and incubators.

## 2. Pain points
- A new subscription for each tool as the team grows.
- Investors, advisers and candidates use different calendars.
- NDAs and offer letters slow deals down.
- Decisions from calls are lost.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Free Basic plan (25 people, 90 minutes); paid plans from A$2 a month, priced by meeting size.
- Guests join free from a link; calendars from Google, Microsoft, Yahoo and iCloud connect once.
- Co-edit decks, notes and spreadsheets in the meeting; sign NDAs as PDFs; webinars for launches and demo days; AI minutes approved by a person.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Lawyer review of NDAs, offer letters and investor documents.
- Records of board and investor decisions.
- Protection of customer data from interviews.

## 5. Gaps to build
- CRM and data-room connectors (HubSpot, Notion, Carta).
- Investor update templates and sharing.
- Startup pricing or credits.

## 6. Starter templates
Investor update; customer interview guide; advisory board agenda; NDA.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
HubSpot, Notion, Slack, Stripe, Zapier.

## 8. Landing page copy
**Headline:** Investors, customers and hires, in one free account.
