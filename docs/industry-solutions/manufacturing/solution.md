# iSift for Manufacturing

Public page: `#/industry/manufacturing` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Small and mid-size manufacturers, distributors, engineering firms, with suppliers and customers across time zones.

## 2. Pain points
- Supplier and customer calls across sites and shifts.
- Quotes, specs and approvals pass by email.
- Office and floor teams use different tools.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Suppliers and customers join free from a link.
- Several people share screens at once for drawings and specs.
- Co-edit spreadsheets and notes live (BOMs, schedules).
- PDF signing for quotes and purchase approvals.
- Cloud recording for training; AI action items you approve.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Quality and safety records (ISO 9001/45001 where certified); controlled documents.
- Export-control and IP confidentiality (NDAs).
- WHS meeting and toolbox-talk records.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Large-file / CAD sharing and markup.
- Mobile-friendly floor join; phone dial-in.
- ERP connectors (MYOB, Xero, NetSuite) and quoting tools.
- Controlled-document versioning and approval.

## 6. Starter templates
Supplier review; toolbox talk; quote approval pack; non-conformance review; production meeting.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
MYOB, Xero, NetSuite, Microsoft 365, Zapier.
