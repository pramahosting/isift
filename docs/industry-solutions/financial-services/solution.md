# iSift for Financial Services

Public page: `#/industry/financial-services` (code: `frontend/src/Industries.tsx`). Landing section: `#industries`.

## 1. Who it is for
Financial advisers, mortgage brokers, accountants and bookkeepers, insurance brokers, small lenders.

## 2. Pain points
- Client meetings, file notes and signed documents are in separate systems.
- Advice and fact-find records must be complete and retained.
- Clients will not install software.

## 3. How iSift fits
- Client joins free from a link.
- AI minutes drafted as file-note starters; adviser approves.
- PDF signing for engagement letters and authorities.
- Booking pages; mail from own firm address; SSO and audit log on Business.

## 4. Sector requirements (verify)
- Record-keeping duties for advice (e.g. ASIC / Corporations Act; credit licensees under NCCP), commonly 7 years.
- Best-interests duty and file notes for advice.
- AML/CTF customer identification (and tax-practitioner / TPB rules for accountants).
- Privacy Act; Consumer Data Right context if handling data feeds.
- Recording consent and storage.

## 5. Gaps to build
- Signing audit trail and templates; ID-verification integration.
- Retention and legal hold; export of full client file.
- Connectors: Xero, MYOB, Xplan, Mortgage Wizard, Salesforce.
- Compliance-ready exports of minutes with approval trail.

## 6. Starter templates
Discovery meeting agenda; fact-find; engagement letter; statement-of-advice review minutes; annual review.

## 7. Integrations to prioritise
Xero, MYOB, Xplan, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier.
