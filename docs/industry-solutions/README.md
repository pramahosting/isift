# iSift Industry Solutions

One folder per sector, modelled on how Zoom (Education, Financial Services, Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing) and Zoho (Real Estate, Nonprofit, Retail, Healthcare, Education, Manufacturing and more) present industry pages: same product, packaged with sector-specific use cases, templates, compliance notes and integrations.

## Sectors

| Folder | Sector | Seen at |
|---|---|---|
| real-estate | Real estate (sales, property management) | Zoho |
| healthcare | Clinics, allied health, aged care | Zoom, Zoho |
| education | Schools, RTOs, universities, tutors | Zoom, Zoho |
| financial-services | Advisers, brokers, accountants, lenders | Zoom, Zoho |
| government | Councils, agencies, public bodies | Zoom |
| manufacturing | Manufacturers and distributors | Zoom, Zoho |
| nonprofit | Charities and community groups | Zoho |
| retail | Retail and e-commerce | Zoho |
| professional-services | Legal, consulting, recruitment | Zoho |
| construction | Builders and trades | Zoho |
| podcasters | Podcasters and creators (uses Podcast Studio) | Requested |
| startups | Founders and small teams | Requested |

## Each sector file follows `_TEMPLATE.md`
1. Who it is for
2. Pain points
3. How iSift fits (only features in the Playbook)
4. Sector requirements (Australia; verify with advisers before publishing)
5. Gaps to build
6. Starter templates
7. Integrations to prioritise
8. Landing page copy
9. Launch checklist

## Rules
- Claims about iSift features must match `iSift-Playbook.pdf`. Items under "Gaps to build" are NOT shipped; do not market them.
- Regulatory notes are general information, not legal advice. Have each reviewed before public use.
- Prices are A$ per host per month: Basic A$0, Standard from A$2, Business from A$7.92.
