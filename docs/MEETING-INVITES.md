# Meeting invitations, replies, host code and pulling people in

## Invitations and replies
- An invitation is one email per person. It carries the calendar entry inline (so Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail show Accept / Maybe / Decline and add the meeting to the person's calendar) and the same `.ics` file as an attachment.
- The email also has personal links: **Accept**, **Maybe**, **Decline**, **Suggest another day or time**, and **Add to calendar** links for Google, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365 and Yahoo. The reply page (`#/rsvp/…`) needs no account.
- Every reply is emailed to the host and listed under **Replies** on the meeting (Meetings, then the meeting). When someone suggests another time, **Use this time** moves the meeting and sends everyone an updated invitation. Replies start again after a move.
- A reply made inside Gmail or Outlook goes to the host's own mailbox as that program's normal reply. iSift lists the replies made on its own reply page.

## Host code
- Each iSift meeting has a private **host code** (for example `K7M2-9QXA`), separate from the guest passcode. It is shown to the host on the meeting (Meetings, then the meeting) and in the meeting's information panel.
- A host who opens the link in a browser that is not signed in chooses **Enter your host code** on the join page and is recognised as the host. Signing in as the host also works.
- For recording to the cloud, sign in as the host.

## Pulling someone into a running meeting
- In a meeting, **Invite** sends each person a personal link that takes them straight in (no passcode, no waiting room) for 12 hours. People with an iSift account also get an alert in iSift. Double-click a name in "Pick from your people" to invite that person at once.
- Meetings that need a signed-in account still ask the person to sign in.

## Recording
- When a recording ends, the host's page stays open on a closing screen with the **Save to this computer** button, or, for a cloud recording, the message that a download link was emailed. If the app has no sender set up, the link goes from the host's own connected mail account.

## iSift inside a meeting
- **iSift apps** on the meeting toolbar opens all of iSift beside the meeting. To show it to everyone, choose **Open in a new tab**, press **Share** and pick that tab.
