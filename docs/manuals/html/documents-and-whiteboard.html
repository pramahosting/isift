iSIFT USER MANUAL

Documents, PDF signing and whiteboard

Write, calculate, present, sign and sketch without leaving iSift.

  1. WordPerfect documents
  2. SpreadTable spreadsheets
  3. PowerPresentation slides
  4. Sign and edit PDFs
  5. Whiteboard

1WordPerfect documents

Docs, WordPerfect. Press New to write with headings, lists, tables and links. Documents are private until you share them and save automatically.

2SpreadTable spreadsheets

SpreadTable works with formulas, several sheets and CSV import and export.

3PowerPresentation slides

Make slides, present them in a meeting, print and share.

4Sign and edit PDFs

Choose a PDF or Word file, edit its text, delete or replace pictures, draw a signature or sign with your own certificate. The file stays in your browser and is not stored on the server.

Good to know: PDFs of up to 5 pages are free.

5Whiteboard

Sketch ideas. Boards save automatically and can be shared in a meeting with everyone.
