Booking, minutes and action items

Let people book time with you, then turn each meeting into reviewed minutes, owned action items and a morning briefing.

  1. Your booking page
  2. Meetings list and minutes
  3. Action items
  4. Morning briefing

1Your booking page

Go to Meetings, Booking. Create a booking page, set your weekly hours, meeting length and notice. People pick a free time with no account and get a video link.

Good to know: Mail must be connected for confirmation emails.

2Meetings list and minutes

Meetings that have a transcript can have minutes drafted. Open Minutes to see each meeting and its status.

Good to know: Minutes are drafts. A person reviews and approves them before they go out.

3Action items

Action items from approved minutes appear here with an owner and a due date, so follow-up does not depend on someone's notebook.

4Morning briefing

Briefings prepare you for the day: what is on and what is still open. Use Preview to see one without sending it.
