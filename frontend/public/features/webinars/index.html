Frequently asked questions

Are webinars included in iSift?

Webinars are part of the paid plans. The free Basic plan doesn't include them.

Can attendees speak?

Attendees only watch, unless you invite them to speak. You can mute them or return them to watching at any time.

Do I need a separate webinar product?

No. Webinars run in the same account as your meetings, mail and documents.
