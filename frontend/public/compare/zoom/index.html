Keep what you already use

Connect Google, Microsoft, Yahoo and iCloud calendars once. Invitations go from your own email address, and Yes / No / Maybe replies come back to your inbox. You can still book meetings on other platforms from one place.

Frequently asked questions

How long are free meetings on iSift?
Free group meetings on iSift run for 90 minutes. Zoom free group meetings are 40 minutes.
Do guests need an account to join iSift?
No. Guests join from a link in their browser, free, with no app to install and no account to create.
Can I keep my calendars?
Yes. Connect Google, Microsoft, Yahoo and iCloud once, and meetings from each calendar appear in iSift.
How much does iSift cost?
The Basic plan is free. Standard starts from A$2 per host a month for a 10-person room, and a 100-person room is A$13.59.
